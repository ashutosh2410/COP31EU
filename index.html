    How this desk works

    Sources and method

    What counts as credible

    Official texts first: the COP31 Presidency, UNFCCC, Council of the EU, European Commission and national governments. Then wire services (Reuters, AFP), specialist outlets (Carbon Brief, Climate Home, IISD, Agence Europe) and established press. Each item carries its tier.

    How sessions get here

    A session appears once its host has published it. EU-linked means organised or co-organised by an EU institution, member-state government or EU-based body. Day placement is our thematic match until the host publishes a time, and is labelled that way.

    Refresh

    A scheduled task re-checks sources every morning and adds new sessions, speakers, registration links and news. When the UNFCCC and pavilion programmes publish times, sessions move onto their real day.
